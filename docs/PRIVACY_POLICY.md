# CleanX privacy policy

Last updated: September 13, 2026.

CleanX replaces selected media on x.com with placeholders. It processes the
rendered page in your browser to identify media, authors, the page title, and
current-page counts. Those page details are not stored or sent anywhere.

The extension saves only your three media switches and username whitelist in
Chrome's local extension storage. These preferences stay on your device and are
removed when you uninstall CleanX. CleanX does not use analytics, advertising,
tracking, accounts, external fonts, remote code, or a backend service.

CleanX makes no network requests. X itself may still load or buffer media that
CleanX hides, and X's own privacy practices continue to apply to use of x.com.

The `storage` permission saves your preferences. `declarativeContent` enables
the toolbar action on X. Host access is restricted to `https://x.com/*` to read
and modify the rendered media there. CleanX does not request access to other
websites, passwords, cookies, messages APIs, or browsing history.

Questions or reports: [CleanX GitHub issues](https://github.com/viseshrp/cleanx/issues).
